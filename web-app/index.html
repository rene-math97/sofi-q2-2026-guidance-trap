The Setup

Four Things That Are True Going In

Every figure below is sourced. Where sources disagree, both numbers are shown. Where a number is my own arithmetic rather than a published figure, it is marked computed.

01

The Operating Business Is Not the Problem

Q1 2026 was a record quarter on almost every line: adjusted net revenue $1,087.2M, up 41% year over year; GAAP net income $166.7M, up 134%; adjusted EBITDA $339.9M at a 31% margin; a record 1,055,000 member adds to 14.7M total, up 35%; record total originations of $12.18B, up 68%. It was the tenth consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability and the eighteenth straight quarter with a Rule of 40 score above 40 (it printed 72%).[1] Tangible book value per share reached $7.21, up 57% year over year.[1] The stock fell 15.4% on that report, from a $18.36 close to $15.525 computed from official exchange closes.[25]

02

The Market Is Trading the Guide, Not the Quarter

In January, SoFi beat Q4 consensus by a penny and the stock fell 9.4%. In April, it matched Q1 consensus at $0.12, beat on revenue, and the stock fell 15.4% computed.[25] The common factor in both was that full-year guidance was reaffirmed rather than raised.[10] FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of ~$0.60 has now been carried unchanged since it was first issued on January 30, 2026.[1][3] That is the trap: the bar for the quarter is low and the bar for the guide is high, and only one of them moves the stock.

03

Two Real Overhangs Remain Unresolved

Technology Platform is shrinking. Q1 segment revenue was $75.1M, down 27% year over year, with accounts down 16% to 132.9M after a large client fully transitioned off the platform before December 31, 2025. Contribution margin collapsed from 30% to 16%.[1] Management still guides to ~$325M for the full year, which requires roughly $83M per quarter across Q2 to Q4 against $75.1M in Q1 computed.[2] The Muddy Waters allegations are unaddressed in the numbers. The March 17 report alleged at least $312M of unrecorded debt and a true personal-loan charge-off rate near 6.1% against a reported 2.89%.[8] SoFi called the report misleading and inaccurate and signaled legal action; the CEO bought 28,900 shares the same day at an average of $17.32.[16] No SEC inquiry, restatement, or auditor action has been disclosed in any filing since.[19]

04

The Guidance Was Built for a Hawkish Fed

This is the underappreciated part of Wednesday. On the Q1 call, CFO Chris Lapointe stated the outlook assumes "an interest rate outlook consistent with the Fed funds futures and no rate cuts in 2026."[2] The FY2026 guide therefore does not depend on easing. A hawkish Warsh press conference at 2:30pm does not break the operating plan. What it can do is compress the multiple, and the multiple is where all the damage has been done this year: the forward P/E has fallen while forward EPS estimates have risen.

The H2 Arithmetic — Why the Guide Is the Whole Game
FY2026 adj. EPS guide − ( Q1 actual + Q2 guide midpoint ) = H2 required
$0.60 − ( $0.12 + $0.105 ) = $0.375
  • $0.60 — company FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance, reaffirmed April 29, 2026[1]
  • $0.12 — Q1 2026 actual adjusted diluted EPS[1]
  • $0.105 — midpoint of the $0.10–$0.11 Q2 guide given on the Q1 call[2]
  • $0.375 — implied H2 2026 requirement, equal to 62% of the full-year target computed

Read plainly: SoFi must earn more in the back half of 2026 than it did in the front half by a wide margin, and it must do so while Technology Platform revenue is contracting. That is why a Q2 beat alone does not clear the stock. A beat that does not come with a raise leaves the same back-loaded year intact, which is precisely the setup that produced a 12% drawdown in April.

The Optionality Nobody Is Paying For

SoFi began minting SoFiUSD, described as the first stablecoin issued by a US national bank on a public permissionless blockchain, with a Mastercard partnership for settlement across global payment networks announced in March 2026.[1] It became available to members on May 27, 2026, and Big Business Banking launched July 1, 2026.[18] Galileo processes roughly 8 billion transactions a year, and management's stated ambition is to migrate that volume onto SoFiUSD rails. Crypto products reached 239,509 at March 31, 2026, from zero a year earlier.[1] None of this is in the FY2026 guide in any material way, and none of it is verifiable as a revenue line yet. It is called out here as optionality, not as a number.

The Short Report Barely Moved the Stock

This is worth stating plainly because the popular framing has it backwards. On March 17, the day the Muddy Waters report was published, SOFI closed at $17.37 against a prior close of $17.63 — down 1.5%. The intraday low was $16.48, so it traded down 6.5% at worst and recovered most of it. Over the following three sessions it drifted to $16.90, roughly 4.1% cumulative.[25]

By the time the report landed, SOFI was already down 33.65% year to date — a figure that computes exactly from official closes of $26.18 on December 31, 2025 and $17.37 on March 17 computed, and which matches the number Muddy Waters itself cited. The stock had been de-rating since January. The Q1 earnings reaction of −15.4% was roughly ten times the size of the short-report reaction. Whatever one concludes about the allegations, the market did not price them as decisive. It priced the guidance.

Why this matters on Wednesday: the personal-loan net charge-off rate is the single line item where the two narratives collide directly. A Q2 print at or below 3.0% with all-in losses holding near 4.4% is the strongest available rebuttal to the short case. A move above roughly 3.3% hands the short thesis a data point it has not had.

Transmission — What Actually Reaches SoFi
  • Guidance is already hawkish-proof. The FY2026 outlook assumes no rate cuts in 2026.[2] A hold or even a hike does not invalidate the operating plan management has been defending all year. This is the most important thing to understand about Wednesday's two events: they are less connected than they look.
  • Deposit costs are the real channel. SoFi pays up for deposits, including a SoFi Plus relaunch offering 4.5% APY on balances up to $20,000 plus a 1% match on Invest and crypto purchases.[1] Higher-for-longer keeps that cost elevated. Offsetting it, deposits still fund the book 155bps cheaper than warehouse lines.[1]
  • Credit is the tail risk. Sustained restrictive policy into a 4%-handle CPI is the environment in which personal-loan losses normalise upward. That is the same line the short report attacks.
  • The multiple is the fast channel. A 2.15-beta, 26× forward earnings name is a duration asset. Multiple compression from a hawkish presser can swamp anything in the 7am release, in either direction.

What Would Make This Wrong

Risks in Both Directions

The short report is directionally right

Med-High

If reported charge-offs materially understate economic losses, then every earnings figure on this page is overstated and the multiple debate is irrelevant. Nothing in the public record adjudicates this either way.

What would settle it: a Q2 charge-off print consistent with the Q1 trend, and continued absence of any auditor, SEC, or restatement disclosure.

Technology Platform does not recover

Med-High

The FY guide needs roughly $83M per quarter from a segment that just printed $75.1M and is losing accounts. If Q2 comes in flat, the ~$325M full-year figure becomes unreachable and the credibility of the whole guidance framework takes damage.

Mitigant: the decline traces to one identified client departure completed in 2025, so the year-over-year comparison should ease from Q1 2026 onward.

A hawkish Fed compresses the multiple regardless

Medium

A 2.15-beta name at 26× forward earnings is exposed to a repricing of duration. A hike, or a hold delivered with hawkish language at 2:30pm, can override a good print in the same session.

Mitigant: guidance already assumes no cuts in 2026, so the operating plan does not need easing to work.

The bear case on lending mix

Medium

The market prefers fee revenue to balance-sheet lending revenue, and the Q1 mix shifted toward lending as Technology Platform shrank. If that continues, the multiple compresses even with earnings growing.

Counterpoint: fee-based revenue still grew 23% to $386.8M, and interchange rose 54% on roughly $25B of annualised spend.

Upside risk: a raise re-rates the stock fast

Low-Med

This cuts both ways. The stock is 48% below its November 2025 high with consensus at Hold and an average target near $20.58 to $22.83 depending on vendor. A genuine guidance raise into a beaten-down positioning base is the setup that produces outsized upside moves.

Evidence: options are pricing roughly an 8.7% to 11.5% move, versus a trailing four-quarter average absolute move near 8.5%.

Model risk in this page itself

Medium

The scenario engine's FY2027 EPS presets under the raise and cut cases are my estimates, not sourced consensus. The multiples are judgement. Two reasonable people can move both sliders and reach opposite conclusions, which is the point of exposing them.

Guardrail: only the $0.81 street FY2027 figure and the $16.88 price are sourced inputs. Everything else in the engine is labelled as an assumption.
